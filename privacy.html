NEXT CHAPTER COLLECTIVE

Your information.
Handled with care.

This notice describes how Next Chapter Collective uses information you provide through this website’s learning and mentoring enquiry form.

What you share

The form asks for your name, email address, learning or mentoring interest, preferred program, preferred mentor, optional company and role, and a description of your goals or challenge. Please avoid including passwords, financial account details, employer confidential information or sensitive personal information.

How we use it

We use your enquiry to respond, understand what support you are looking for, discuss mentor fit and arrange a potential engagement. Submitting an enquiry does not sign you up for marketing emails.

Who may see it

The people coordinating enquiries and relevant founding mentors may review information needed to discuss your request. Website hosting and email providers process information as needed to operate the website and deliver the enquiry.

Retention and your choices

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to manage the conversation and any resulting engagement, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. To request access, a correction or deletion, reply to any email you receive from us about your enquiry. If you have not received a reply, use the enquiry form and identify your request without including unnecessary personal information.

Website operation

This website does not include advertising trackers, analytics scripts or marketing cookies. Its form handler uses short-term submission counters associated with a hashed network address to limit repeated submissions. The counters contain no enquiry message. The hosting provider may maintain operational and security logs under its own policies.

Mentoring engagements

If we proceed with mentoring, we will discuss the scope, confidentiality and any additional information needed before the engagement begins.
