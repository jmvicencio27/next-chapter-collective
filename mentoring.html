A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Do I need to know exactly what help I need?

No. Tell us what feels stuck or what you want to change. We can use that as the starting point to discuss a suitable mentoring engagement.

Can you help us choose business technology?

We can help you clarify the problem, assess priorities and consider technology options. The right mentor depends on your needs. Software development, installation and other implementation work require a separate scope.

Can I choose my mentor?

You can share a preference in your enquiry. We’ll discuss fit and availability before confirming your engagement.

Are sessions online or in person?

Online sessions are the starting format. If you need an in-person or team engagement, mention it in your enquiry so we can discuss what is possible.

Will mentoring guarantee business growth or a promotion?

Mentoring helps you make more informed decisions and take action. Results depend on your circumstances and follow-through; business results, promotions and job placements are not guaranteed.
